## Prompt da ilustração

> Create a polished editorial educational illustration, wide landscape 16:9, for a university lecture about edge computing, cloud computing and digital twins of urban bus fleets. A realistic Brazilian city avenue with one modern white and burgundy bus in foreground on the left, tiny embedded edge computer visible through a tasteful technical cutaway near bus dashboard. Right half shows the SAME urban blocks and bus mirrored as a translucent cyan wireframe digital twin, with restrained data links rising toward a distant cloud data center. White/light warm background, deep navy, burgundy red and teal accents, precise architectural isometric perspective, premium textbook visual, detailed but uncluttered, no text, no letters, no logos, no labels. Conceptual illustration, not a real city photograph. Fill the canvas with the scene and leave margins. Output should be saved as an image asset.
